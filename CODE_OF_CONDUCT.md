# Code of Conduct

BUGS! Video Editor is a small project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented, is asked to keep it a pleasant place to work.

## What we expect

* Be kind and patient with everyone, whatever their experience.
* Respect other people's opinions, and disagree politely.
* Give helpful feedback, and take feedback well.
* Own your mistakes and put them right.
* Keep words and pictures clean and decent.
* Stay on topic: this is a place to talk about the editor.

## What is not allowed

* Insults, mockery, trolling or personal attacks.
* Harassing or pestering anyone, in public or in private.
* Crude or offensive language or images.
* Sharing someone's private details, such as their address or email, without their permission.
* Political or religious arguments.

## Reporting a problem

Tell the maintainer privately: open this repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say it is a conduct report. Only the maintainer can see it. Every report is looked at promptly and fairly, and the person who reported it is kept private.

## What happens

The maintainer may remove, edit or turn down comments, code, issues and other contributions that break this code. Depending on how serious it is and whether it keeps happening, someone may get:

1. **A private reminder** explaining what was wrong.
2. **A warning**, with a break from talking to the people involved.
3. **A temporary ban** from the project.
4. **A permanent ban** from the project.
