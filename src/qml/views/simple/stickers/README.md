# Collage stickers

Still Noto Emoji images (2D, 512 px) from https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji, copyright
Google LLC. The project's README says its image resources are under the Apache License 2.0 and
its fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; its top-level LICENSE file is the OFL. Both texts
are here.

`stickers.json` is the **allow-list**. BUGS! shows only the stickers listed there, in that order,
whatever else is in this folder or in Shotcut's `elements/`.

Every sticker on the list was checked by eye to be appropriate and tznius. Nothing goes on the
list without the same check. Left out on purpose:

- romance and kissing, and people or relationships against the Torah, including same-sex
  couples and families and pride or transgender flags
- immodest clothing
- non-kosher food, including meat with cheese
- other religions' symbols
- Halloween and the occult
- gambling, weapons and crude gestures
- the evil-eye bead

To add one, copy its PNG from `2D/png/512/` here as `<code points>.png`, then add it to
`stickers.json`.
